Scheduler & Quick Actions

What do Heartbeat, Morning briefing & Nightly cleanup mean?
  • Heartbeat: Periodic silent background check (watches deadlines & messages per HEARTBEAT.md; notifies you only when something is urgent).
  • Morning briefing: Daily morning summary of your schedule, priorities, and open tasks (cron format, e.g. 0 8 * * * = daily at 8:00).
  • Nightly cleanup & reset: Saves important facts from today's chats into long-term memory at night and clears old context (saves tokens and keeps replies sharp).
